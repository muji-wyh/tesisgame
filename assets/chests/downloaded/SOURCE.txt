Downloaded chest artwork for the shared reward presentation
=========================================================

Five source-derived designs extend the three original animated chest types.
Each of the eight themes now has a distinct design:

Spring: Royal (existing 2D rig)
Summer: Energy (existing 2D rig)
Autumn: Harvest Wood (Casual Chests, original CHEST_T3 and openT3 clip)
Winter: Crystal (existing nine-part 2D rig)
Ocean: Tide Blue (Low Poly Chest Animated, original blue chest/opening clip)
Space: Nebula Vault (Stylized 3D Animated Chests, original Epic Chest)
Jungle: Bramble Chest (POLY STYLE Fantasy Treasure Chest, Treasure_Chest_3a)
Candy: Bonbon Barrel (POLY STYLE Fantasy Treasure Chest, Treasure_Chest_2a)

Original source packages were downloaded to C:/uworks/TalkQuest/downloads.
The adjacent manifest records their Unity Asset Store product IDs, publishers,
versions and complete package SHA-256 values, plus export/frame checksums.
The source packages remain outside the repository. The derived PNG frames and
their import sidecars are private local build inputs excluded from Git. They
ship as embedded game content under the Unity Asset Store Standard EULA, not a
separate redistributable asset pack. Publisher scripts and controller behavior are not
included or executed by this game.

Geometry and materials came from the previously verified Unity prefab exports
under C:/uworks/TalkQuest/asset-review/media. All five designs keep their source
meshes, material colors, UVs and supported textures. Blender Principled shading,
transparent background and consistent orthographic lighting approximate the
Unity materials; custom publisher shaders and demo scene effects are not copied.

Harvest uses all nine existing samples of the publisher's opening animation.
Tide uses the first seven source samples, stopping in the open pose before the
closing half of the publisher's open/close clip. Nebula has an authored magnetic
lift of the separate source lid. Bramble and Bonbon have authored hinge rotation
from closed to the publisher's 60-degree open pose using their source pivots.
The latter three motions are game adaptations, not claims about supplied clips.

Rebuild the three authored model sequences with Blender:
  blender --background --factory-startup --disable-autoexec --python
    tools/prepare-downloaded-chests.py -- --render

Then pack the five designs and verify the source archives:
  python tools/prepare-downloaded-chests.py --pack

Both commands support --source-root to relocate the existing TalkQuest archive.
The baker keeps its intermediate frames and review contact sheet in build/chest-bakes.
The 43 runtime frames are lossless RGBA PNGs at 320 x 320, totaling under 3 MB.

The game drives these opening poses with the same ChestView hold confirmation,
release timing, sounds, body weight, light, cancellation, surprise and reduced
motion behavior as the existing chest designs. There is no whole-frame dissolve.
