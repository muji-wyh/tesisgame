Animated model artwork for the shared reward presentation
========================================================

Five distinct source models replace the former 320 x 320 opening sequences:

Autumn: Harvest Ironwood - Casual Chests, CHEST_2_T3 (Aztech Games)
Ocean: Tide Captain - Stylized Sea Chest (Project Zima)
Space: Nebula Crown - Stylized 3D Animated Chests, Crowned Chest (Bobardo)
Jungle: Bramble Relic - POLY STYLE Fantasy Treasure Chest, family 5a (Batata Studio)
Candy: Bonbon Gold - Animated Cartoon Treasure Chest (Flying Saci Game Studio)

Spring Royal, Summer Energy, and Winter Crystal retain their original 2D rigs.
The Talk Quest reward subclass retains its separately authored mechanisms.

The models come from acquired free packages retained under C:/uworks/TalkQuest.
The adjacent version 2 manifest records each publisher, original package hash,
source-model hash, source/runtime texture sizes, converted GLB hash, and the
motion/material adaptation. The source Unity packages remain outside this
repository. Licensed GLBs and their import sidecars are ignored private build
inputs and ship only inside the compiled game pack under the Unity Asset Store
Standard EULA. They are not distributed as a reusable asset library. Publisher
scripts, controllers, and demo scene behaviors are not imported or executed.

Sea Chest preserves its original UVs and base/normal/PBR detail, with 4K source
maps reduced to 2K runtime maps. Its shader graph maps green to roughness and
blue to metallic. The source quaternion animation opens its lid and moves its
lock and handles. Custom Unity color/dirt modifiers remain approximate.

The Cartoon chest preserves its original hand-painted 512 pixel atlas without
upscaling. Its embedded Open take supplies the continuous opening motion.
The three other designs preserve their source silhouettes with adapted material
response and edge treatment. Their per-design motion provenance is in the
manifest; authored hinge motion is identified separately from source clips.

ChestModelView renders the real geometry with studio reflections, shadows, and
an interior light into an adaptive 512-1024 square viewport. ChestView places
this texture using stable 1024-unit design coordinates. There are no sparse
opening-frame swaps. The caller controls animation sampling, so visibility,
pause, cancellation, and reduced motion use the same reward lifecycle as the
original chests. Viewports stop updating while hidden or paused.

Rebuild all five private models using Blender 5.2:
  blender --background --factory-startup --disable-autoexec --python tools/prepare-chest-models.py --
  python tools/prepare-chest-models.py --optimize
  node tools/package-chest-models.cjs
  npm run import

The converter accepts --source-root, --output, and --work. The packager accepts
the TalkQuest root as its first positional argument. Previews and intermediate
maps stay under the ignored build/chest-quality directory. Run
tests/godot/chest_models_visual_review.gd with a graphics renderer and
--audio-driver Dummy for silent in-game close-ups and animation captures;
it never touches player saves.
