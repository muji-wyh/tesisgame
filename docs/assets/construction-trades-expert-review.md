# Construction & Trades expert-review brief

## Status and scope

The complete draft contains 500 distinct canonical senses and 100 expressions: the shared 100/20 Foundation plus 400 specialist memberships and 80 specialist expressions. Each module has 50 senses and 10 expressions. The specialist expansion creates 311 senses and reuses 89. Independent editorial review addressed physical-action illustration briefs, natural workplace expressions, the test-certificate definition, the crane-operator role, and the shared specification meaning. It does not constitute qualified trade, language, morphology, visual, or regional approval. All required release reviews remain pending, and no new media has been acquired.

All 400 specialist senses have a scored route inside their own module. The 209 new pending morphology graphs contain 197 composites and 12 explicit atomic decisions. The other 102 analyses remain null. Every null or atomic sense has a genuine same-module Phrase focus; atomic exposure does not count as scored practice by itself. Structural reconstruction and route coverage do not prove that an analysis is linguistically or professionally appropriate.

The course teaches communication about drawings, site information, materials, work allocation, records, and coordination. It does not teach a construction method, tool operation, electrical connection, hazardous-energy isolation, excavation procedure, lifting technique, load calculation, protective-equipment selection, or legal approval. Local roles, authorization, contract definitions, building requirements, and inspection powers must be reviewed for the intended launch regions. Normal workplace exchanges are kept concise; professional-review limits remain in the accompanying metadata.

## Review assignments

| Area | Required decisions |
|---|---|
| Workplaces and equipment | Verify workshop, material store, laydown area, access tower, scaffold, work platform, tools, and plant at gameplay size. The physical workshop differs from a training session; a scaffold structure differs from the teaching verb. A construction material store is not automatically the retail stockroom sense. Confirm visual distinctions among a square, spirit level, laser level, trowel, float, drill, and drill bit. |
| Materials and components | Review cement, concrete, mortar, grout, aggregate, sealant, adhesive, insulation, membrane, fasteners, beam, joist, and lintel. Preserve distinct material identities and applications. Aggregate here is granular construction material, not data summarization. A depicted component or certificate cannot establish compatibility, strength, or suitability. |
| Actions | Verify the actual verb meanings of mark, drill, sand, grind, saw, weld, solder, coat, seal, align, level, assemble, dismantle, erect, excavate, compact, cure, lay, install, connect, disconnect, anchor, and lower. Welding may use heat, pressure, or both; it is not restricted to fusion. Repair stays distinct from re + pair. The reused pour sense concerns a liquid; secure concerns goods handling. |
| Drawings and dimensions | Check plan, elevation, section, detail, scale, symbol, legend, datum, references, and measurement units. Distinguish diameter from radius, nominal from actual size, and linear from area or volume units. Flush means even with an adjacent surface, not merely horizontal. Load-bearing classification requires the actual design; non-load-bearing does not mean carrying no load or permission to remove a partition. |
| Planning and work stages | Verify work package, sequence, access, permit-to-work, method statement, hold point, witness point, temporary works, formwork, falsework, service information, curing, drying, testing, commissioning, and completion. A permit is not a complete control system. Witness-point arrangements depend on the applicable plan. Installation completion does not establish verified system performance. |
| Risks and protection | Review dust, silica, asbestos, noise, vibration, manual handling, work at height, confined space, excavation collapse, hot work, fire watch, and electrical hazards with qualified reviewers. No appearance alone identifies asbestos or establishes exposure. PPE descriptions do not select equipment, certify fit, or imply universal protection. The reused assembly point remains tied to site-building evacuation. |
| Quality and records | Verify nonconformity, corrosion, crack, test and inspection records, calibration evidence, competence records, drawing issue, revision status, and sign-off. A test certificate certifies results of specified tests within its stated scope; it is not any conformity document. An observation is not automatically a defect diagnosis, structural conclusion, or instruction to repair. |
| Submissions and commercial information | Distinguish a request for information from a technical submittal, and a change notice from an authorized change order. Review material/sample approval, bill of quantities, cost estimate, and measurement record under actual project arrangements. A drawing register, transmittal, or approval label does not establish that the underlying document is current or sufficient. |
| Roles and project completion | Verify each trade role, surveyor, quantity surveyor, engineer, architect, contractor, supervisor, inspector, and officer within local appointment boundaries. Crane operator is a person whose work is operating a crane; permission alone does not define the occupation. Review practical completion, defects liability period, final inspection, occupancy permit, and client acceptance against their governing arrangements. None implies universal legal consequences. |
| Canonical reuse | HR owns the shared floor plan and apprentice; IT owns the asset register; the handover meeting is a shared coordination event. Education paintbrush, cut, broad paint verb, and daily report meanings are reused. Physical dimension and prerequisite dependency use their corrected shared senses. Keep project progress reports distinct from reports about learning goals. |
| Shared specification | Confirm the clarified shared definition: a detailed description of required characteristics, performance, or arrangements for a product, system, component, or work. This covers material, size, finish, and work requirements as well as system behavior. Its canonical identity, existing Software example, media, and morphology remain intact; Construction contextual domain review is pending. |
| Morphology | Review every explicit boundary and spelling rule. Check fasten + er, tight + en, loose + en, drive + er, tile + er, survey + or, milli/centi + meter, and prefix and hyphen boundaries. Review lexicalized formwork/falsework and trade + s + person for semantic transparency and teaching value. Plumber remains null rather than being inferred from the vertical-alignment meaning of plumb. Uncertain analyses must not become atomic by default. |
| Regional forms and difficulty | Review wrench/spanner, vise/vice, vapor/vapour, center/centre, meter/metre, laborer/labourer, permit and contract terminology, and role titles. Accepted variants are editorial candidates and never extra counted senses. Difficulty labels are provisional rather than certified CEFR outcomes. |
| Visuals and audio | Acquire and inspect appropriate sourced production assets and record original source, creator, license, acquisition status, and available animations. Revised action briefs request visible actions or concrete before/after scenes rather than paper-only representations. Review at gameplay size; curing and other abstract changes may remain unsuitable for isolated picture identification. Audio uses ava-sweet: en-US-AvaNeural, rate -15%, pitch +8Hz, volume +0%, no postprocessing. |

## Consulted sources and limits

Eight source records under `construction-` IDs retain exact URLs, publishers, consultation dates, and scopes in the source registry. They were consulted on 2026-10-16:

- [BLS Construction Laborers and Helpers](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/construction-and-extraction/construction-laborers-and-helpers.htm): occupational duties, workplace context, tools, materials, team support, and training.
- [BLS Carpenters](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/construction-and-extraction/carpenters.htm): duties, tools, plans, measurement, materials, and trade coordination.
- [BLS Electricians](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/construction-and-extraction/electricians.htm): occupational diagrams, components, records, coordination, and training context. Electrical work instructions were not derived from this overview.
- [BLS Plumbers, Pipefitters, and Steamfitters](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/construction-and-extraction/plumbers-pipefitters-and-steamfitters.htm): duties, piping materials and fixtures, plans, estimates, inspections, and building-information-model context. No joining, pressure-test, gas-system, or commissioning method was adopted.
- [BLS Construction and Building Inspectors](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/construction-and-extraction/construction-and-building-inspectors.htm): drawings, specifications, alignment/level observations, staged inspections, records, photographs, and findings. Local inspection authority remains subject to appointment and jurisdiction.
- [HSE Site rules and induction](https://www.hse.gov.uk/construction/safetytopics/site-rules-induction.htm): the page body was read for site-specific information, briefings, reporting, routes, restricted areas, and emergency arrangements.
- [HSE Planning for construction work](https://www.hse.gov.uk/construction/safetytopics/planning.htm): planning and information sections were read for access, services, materials, resources, and coordination. Linked detailed procedures were not reviewed.
- [HSE Health risks](https://www.hse.gov.uk/construction/healthrisks/index.htm): index-level consultation only for welfare, substances, asbestos, silica, noise, vibration, and manual handling. Exposure limits, equipment selection, medical claims, and linked control procedures were not read or adopted.

The BLS occupational pages support contextual relevance rather than a universal qualification or code requirement. The HSE pages provide UK context rather than a global legal template. No consulted source supplies a vocabulary-frequency ranking, certifies every original definition, approves a safe work method, or grants asset rights. All term references remain `pending_term_check`.

Record reviewer identity, relevant expertise, date, region, source edition, exact canonical or expression ID, decision, and revised wording before changing a release gate. Language, domain, morphology, picture suitability, pronunciation, asset provenance, packaging, and complete-theme readiness each require their own evidence. Source previews, downloaded assets, converted assets, and integrated media must remain distinct states.
