# Professional workplace artwork

All twenty small Home previews are integrated: Retail, Software & Data, Home & Elder
Care, Restaurants & Food Service, Office Administration, HR & Recruitment, and
Nursing, Warehousing & Logistics, Hospitality & Tourism, Teaching & Childcare,
Transport & Delivery, Manufacturing & Maintenance, Cleaning & Facilities,
Construction & Trades, IT Support, Customer Support, Accounting & Finance,
Sales & Marketing, Clinical Medicine, and Legal & Paralegal.
They are static illustrations composed from actual acquired Kenney and
Innerscene models, with Perhelion's original scales illustration in the Legal
Office. This does not make any full theme or curriculum module ready.

The exact source files, licenses, dependent textures, hashes, conversion steps,
and visual review are recorded in [the source manifest](professional-themes.json).
The library was supplied locally by the user as Kenney Game Assets All-in-1
3.7.0. Original Innerscene care, facilities, carpentry and electronics-repair
models were downloaded under explicit CC0 dedication. The original CC0 scales
SVG was acquired from Wikimedia Commons. No purchase was performed.

The twenty compositions use 88 distinct source model files with their original
materials and one original SVG wall illustration, for 89 source assets.
Floors and walls are source models, as are all furnishings and equipment. The
source pack's promotional preview images are inspection references and are not
used as scene artwork. Static lighting and an orthographic camera were applied
in Godot, then the 1280 x 800 render was reduced with Lanczos filtering. No source
geometry was replaced by procedural stand-ins.

Codex visually inspected the final 512 x 320 previews and 296 x 185 phone images
on 2026-10-16. This is a recorded Codex visual review, not a claim of human
approval, occupational accuracy review, or physical-device testing. Rendering
used Windows ANGLE with Microsoft Basic Render Driver. Retail retains distinct
shelves, checkout, cart, basket, bread, fruit, and freezer silhouettes. Software
retains two computer workstations.

Welcoming Home uses a domestic sitting area with a visibly usable wheelchair
beside the seating. Its source scale was corrected to match the furniture after
the first render. It is an introductory domestic setting, not a certification of
accessibility clearances or a clinical care scene. Restaurant & Kitchen combines
two dining tables and plated meals with visible preparation equipment. Contact
heights for the plates, coffee machine and table lamp were corrected from actual
source bounds before final review. Both new compositions and their final phone
images were inspected on 2026-10-16. No characters or care/food procedures are
depicted by these still previews.

Office Hub has one corner workstation, document storage, and a separate small
coordination table. People & Interview Office centers on a three-seat interview
table with a separate waiting bench. Their compositions differ from the paired
workstations in Digital Studio. Tabletop contacts were derived from the acquired
meshes' bounds. Codex inspected both final 512 x 320 previews and their 296 x 185
phone versions on 2026-10-16. These are introductory settings; they do not depict
or certify any legal or HR procedure.

Care Ward uses an actual acquired hospital-bed model with its raised backrest,

The source directory is excluded from Web exports. The bed has no baked clips
or rig; independent mechanical meshes do not imply working articulation.
The scene lowers key lighting to preserve the pale equipment's edges. Codex
inspected the final 512 x 320 and 296 x 185 phone images on 2026-10-16. This
introductory still depicts no procedure and is not clinical approval of the

All five compositions were inspected at 1280 x 800, 512 x 320 and 296 x 185.

canonical pixels differed from their standalone scratch renders and were

vocabulary image, or course readiness.

Career cards now show the same reviewed local previews before selection. Missing
previews remain absent, and all incomplete courses retain preparation status.
The image shares its parent card's pointer and keyboard target. Four-size actual

their texture references are released when leaving the list.

These previews contain no baked animation. Furniture Kit's 140 GLB models have
no animation clips. Mini Market's selected props are static; its unselected

animations were inventoried but were not used or visually reviewed for this
delivery. `theme.preview` is separate from the pending on-demand `art.home`,
`art.environment`, and `art.props` descriptors.

Additional clinical source research on 2026-10-16 found the following candidates.
These three listings remain source-page findings only: no model downloads,
visual acceptance or integration has occurred for them.

- [Atomic Realm Hospital Assets](https://atomicrealm.itch.io/hospital-assets):
  the free tier lists 18 models. The custom license permits commercial use and
  editing, requires author credit, and prohibits asset redistribution, including
  modified assets. Do not copy its raw files into this repository without
  resolving that distribution constraint. The source page states no generative
  AI was used; animations and final style remain uninspected.
- [3DAssets.dev Hospital Wards and Clinic Operations](https://3dassets.dev/packs/hospital-wards-and-clinic-operations):
  the page identifies 80 models by `@3dassets`, a CC0 dedication, and AI use on
  every file. It describes static geometry with named articulation nodes, which
  does not establish playable animations or production quality. Geometry,
  clinical representation and visual fit still require actual inspection.
- [Quaternius Simple Buildings](https://quaternius.com/packs/simplebuildings.html)
  is CC0 but supplies a hospital exterior, so it does not resolve ward equipment.

No paid pack was purchased and no model is approved solely from these listings.

## Reproduce

```text
node tools/render-professional-theme-review.cjs
node tools/prepare-professional-theme-previews.cjs
```

The render command reads the supplied source library, or `KENNEY_LIBRARY` when
set, and reads the acquired sources retained under `docs/assets/sources`.
It writes sixty review PNGs plus a source report under
`build/professional-english/theme-review`. It does not modify production themes.
The import command verifies every recorded source/dependency/license and the
exact approved candidate hashes before copying the twenty PNGs and licenses. A
different renderer may produce different pixels; new bytes require a new visual
review and manifest update. The importer changes only `theme.preview`, preserving
full-theme readiness and other fields.
