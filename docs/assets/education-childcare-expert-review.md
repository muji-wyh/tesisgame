# Teaching & Childcare expert-review brief

## Status and scope

The integrated draft contains 500 distinct canonical senses and 100 expressions: the shared 100/20 foundation plus 400 specialist memberships and 80 specialist expressions. Every module has 50 senses and 10 expressions. The specialist expansion creates 294 senses and reuses 106, including thirteen integrated IT Support identities. Independent semantic and expression review is recorded; all required language, education-domain, morphology, picture-suitability, and production-media approvals remain pending. No module is ready.

All 400 specialist senses have a scored route within their own module. There are 226 new pending graphs: 17 explicit atomic decisions and 209 composites. The 68 null analyses retain contextual Phrase assessment. Every new verb in module05 has contextual verbal use; reused action and safety terms also have local assessment routes. Exact reconstruction and coverage do not establish professional or linguistic accuracy.

This course teaches workplace English for education and childcare communication. It does not teach a care technique, diagnostic threshold, developmental deadline, universal safeguarding procedure, qualification entitlement, or mandatory educational intervention. Difficulty labels and regional alternatives remain editorial candidates. The adult learner's profession does not establish the ages or abilities of the children in any particular setting.

## Review assignments

| Area | Required decisions |
|---|---|
| Learning spaces and materials | Verify classroom, outdoor, sensory, creative, movement, and literacy materials. Illustrations should be welcoming, inclusive, identifiable, and suitable for the intended age context without presenting unattended use, hazardous setup, or a universal staffing arrangement. |
| Development and communication | Review cognition, curiosity, attachment, coordination, memory, balance, developmental milestone, communication, and language concepts. Emotional attachment differs from a linked digital file; human memory differs from computing storage. Developmental timing requires context and must not become a diagnostic judgment. |
| Language units and phonics | Verify phoneme, grapheme, phonemic awareness, phonics, syllable, rhyme, blending, segmenting, decoding, pronunciation, and morphology. Grapheme is scoped to English phonics. Phonemic awareness includes recognizing and working with sounds. Do not infer morphemes from syllable divisions or spelling length. |
| Teaching actions | Confirm assess, model, scaffold, guide, prompt, trace, annotate, estimate, sequence, blend, segment, infer, revise, and reflect in their actual sentence contexts. Educational assessment differs from care assessment; sound blending differs from food mixing; drawing a trace differs from a software trace; sequence has separate noun and verb senses. |
| Planning and assessment | Distinguish learning goal, learning evidence, success criterion, feedback, rubric, portfolio, formative assessment, summative assessment, screening, and diagnosis. No score, assessment protocol, developmental cutoff, or guaranteed outcome is supplied. Review validity, language context, cultural context, and multiple sources of evidence. |
| Families and daily arrangements | Review parent, guardian, interpreter, consent, pickup arrangements, family partnership, transitions, and communication preferences. A family role or record does not by itself establish authority. Translation and interpreting remain distinct, and no family structure is presented as universal. |
| Safeguarding and inclusion | Verify safeguarding concern, disclosure, allegation, designated roles, supervised access, attendance, inclusion, reasonable adjustment, allergy, and support plans. Allegation is not an established fact. Supervised access concerns visitor/classroom access. Local duties, consent, emergency arrangements, and reporting authority require qualified review. No medication, feeding, sleep, lifting, restraint, investigation, or emergency technique is taught. |
| Digital records and accessibility | Review learner records, family messages, permissions, notification, upload, consent records, captions, screen readers, assistive technology, and the filter control. The filter is a noun control here; IT's filtering action is a verb. Fictional artwork must not reveal real child, family, or staff information. |
| Roles and professional learning | Verify mentor, mentee, supervision, probation, qualifications, team briefing, learning communities, reflective practice, professional development, and staffing terminology. Job description reuses the existing canonical role-duty record instead of adding a synonym. A vocabulary lesson does not confer qualification or authorization. |
| Regional scope | Individualized education program is a written educational plan in the U.S. context. Designated safeguarding lead and special educational needs coordinator are marked England. Verify all variants, role meanings, responsibilities, and launch regions independently; they are not universally equivalent. |
| Morphology | Confirm semantic transparency, lexicalization, stems, affixes, compounds, allomorphs, and word boundaries. Preschooler, handwriting, staff-to-child ratio, and dress-up clothes have explicit candidates. Clothes stays intact; it is not clothe plus s. Phoneme, grapheme, syllable, morphology, cognition, and other uncertain analyses remain null. |
| Artwork and voice | Acquire and inspect appropriate production assets, record source, creator, license, acquisition, and animations, and verify meaning at gameplay size. All new picture-suitability flags remain false. Audio retains ava-sweet: en-US-AvaNeural, rate -15%, pitch +8Hz, volume +0%, no postprocessing. |
