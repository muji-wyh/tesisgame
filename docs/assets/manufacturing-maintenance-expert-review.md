# Manufacturing & Maintenance expert-review brief

## Scope and current status

The complete draft has 500 distinct canonical senses and 100 expressions: the shared 100/20 Foundation plus 400 specialist memberships and 80 specialist expressions. Each module contains 50 senses and 10 expressions. The specialist expansion creates 247 senses and reuses 153. Independent editorial review is being coordinated separately from qualified language, manufacturing, maintenance, metrology, safety, morphology, regional, and visual approval. All required release reviews remain pending; no new media has been acquired.

All 400 specialist senses have a scored route inside their own module. The 213 new pending morphology graphs contain 205 composites and 8 explicit atomic decisions. The other 34 analyses remain null. Every null or atomic sense has a real same-module Phrase focus. Structural reconstruction and route coverage do not establish that an analysis is linguistically or professionally appropriate.

The course teaches communication about equipment, components, assigned work, production planning, condition reports, drawings, measurement evidence, and coordination. It does not teach machine operation, cutting settings, CNC programming, electrical work, lubrication quantities, calibration methods, inspection procedures, hazardous-energy isolation, lockout/tagout steps, restart sequences, or legal approval. Regional role boundaries, actual procedures, engineering standards, inspection authority, and release requirements must be reviewed for intended launch contexts.

## Required review decisions

| Area | Required decisions |
|---|---|
| Machinery and workplaces | Verify factory, production line, assembly line, machine shop, machining center, lathe, milling machine, drill press, grinding machine, hydraulic press, injection molding machine, industrial robot, and welding station. Check equipment pictures at gameplay size and distinguish a general production line from its assembly subtype. An equipment name or image does not authorize operation. |
| Tooling and instruments | Distinguish jig from assembly fixture, vise from clamp, cutting tool from drill bit, and torque wrench from an ordinary wrench. Review caliper, micrometer, dial indicator, feeler gauge, pressure gauge, multimeter, infrared thermometer, surface plate, and measuring instrument. Multimeter briefs avoid live probing; infrared temperature is a surface estimate under applicable conditions. |
| Materials and stock forms | Verify raw material, workpiece, blank, casting, forging, billet, sheet metal, steel, stainless steel, aluminum, copper, brass, alloy, rubber, plastic, and composite material. Material appearance cannot establish grade, composition, suitability, or traceability. Casting and forging are selected as resulting objects, rather than the process nouns. |
| Mechanical components | Review bearing, shaft, gear, pulley, drive belt, chain, sprocket, coupling, mechanical seal, gasket, O-ring, nozzle, spring, piston, cylinder, rivet, and the reused fasteners and fluid components. The selected cylinder is a piston chamber. A spring can store energy; a component photograph is not a removal or release method. |
| Electrical and fluid components | Confirm electric motor, hydraulic pump, air compressor, control cabinet, fuse, relay, sensor, and physical filter. A fuse description does not select a rating. A relay is an electrically operated switching device. The generic filter is distinct from data filtering and from the narrower building air-filter subtype. |
| Lubricants | Review lubricant, semisolid grease, coolant, cutting fluid, lubrication system, lubricate, and lubrication. Industrial grease is a lubricant sense, distinct from Cleaning's unwanted oily/fatty soiling. Product names do not establish chemical compatibility, mixing ratios, quantities, or maintenance methods. |
| Actions and work scope | Review maintain, service, repair, assemble, dismantle, install, connect, disconnect, align, tighten, loosen, cut, drill, grind, weld, lubricate, calibrate, deburr, polish, machine, and fabricate as verbs. Preserve their exact parts of speech in expressions. Action briefs should show the action or component relationship rather than only a form. No operating parameters or sequence are taught. |
| Task descriptions | Verify surface preparation, component removal, part replacement, machine setup, tool setting, finishing, heat treatment, metal forming, material handling, equipment cleaning, dimensional/visual inspection, fault diagnosis, and the reused functional/pressure tests. These name work scopes and do not supply methods or validate competence. |
| Production planning | Distinguish production plan, schedule, order, and run; batch size, lot number, product code, and part number; bill of materials, routing sheet, and operation sequence. Work in progress and finished goods use stated production statuses. A finished-goods label does not automatically prove every release requirement. |
| Flow and measures | Review cycle time, takt time, lead time, setup time, changeover, bottleneck, capacity, throughput, output, target, rate, yield, reject rate, scrap rate, and rework. Counted populations, start/end points, periods, inclusion of rework, and denominators must be explicit. Takt time is a planning relationship, not a machine-speed instruction. |
| Production arrangements | Verify kanban as a pull-system signal; distinguish pull from push, flow/job/batch production, pilot run, prototype, and first article. Standard work, work instruction, and process parameter depend on the relevant production system. A prototype differs from an initial production item used for specified verification. |
| Maintenance strategies and records | Review preventive, reactive, predictive, condition-based, scheduled, mechanical, and electrical maintenance. Predictive forecasts are uncertain. Review service life, remaining life, failure mode/analysis, condition monitoring, vibration/oil analysis, overhaul, interval, and recurring fault. Intervals and limits must come from actual requirements. |
| Availability and timing | Preserve the reused equipment-availability sense: readiness of equipment for allocation at the needed time. Do not silently convert it to a reliability ratio. Unplanned downtime, operating time, repair time, and response time need explicit counting rules. Planned shutdown is the stopping event; planned outage is an unavailable period. |
| Maintenance coordination | Confirm maintenance log, repair record, repair history, equipment history, asset register, identifiers, serial number, spare parts inventory, maintenance checklist, and service record. Existing spare part is reused once; replacement-part synonyms are not additional counted senses. Return to service requires the actual checks and designated release rather than repair completion alone. |
| Quality and equipment findings | Review quality control versus quality assurance, acceptance decision, nonconformity, wear, wear and tear, misalignment, imbalance, overheating, blockage, contamination, metal fatigue, burr, surface damage, loose connection, pressure loss, abnormal sound, and out-of-tolerance. Reported symptoms and visible features do not establish cause or acceptance. |
| Hazardous-energy terminology | Review hazardous energy, stored energy, unexpected startup, energy source, isolation point, lockout, tagout, and authorized person under the actual jurisdiction and program. A tag does not physically restrain equipment. Tagout is not universally equivalent to lockout. Emergency stop does not imply all energy is isolated. This draft supplies no control or restart method. |
| Other safety communication | Review guarding, restricted access, hot surface, sharp edge, pinch point, moving/rotating parts, flying debris, risk assessment, risk control, equipment defect report, incident investigation, and near miss. An out-of-service label is a status indicator rather than proof of isolation. Protective arrangements cannot be approved from an isolated image. |
| Geometry and metrology | Verify thickness, clearance, surface roughness, flatness, concentricity, tolerance, nominal/actual size, measurement uncertainty, calibration, certificate, and reference standard. Calibration establishes relationships with references and is not automatically adjustment. Concentricity requires the applicable geometric definition. A calibration certificate is scoped evidence rather than universal fitness-for-use approval. |
| Quality records and charts | Review batch/production records, process sheet, control plan, check sheet, run chart, control chart, sampling plan, sample size, and nonconformance report. A check sheet records observations; a checklist identifies tasks or checks. Statistically derived control limits differ from product tolerance limits. No sampling rules or statistical method is supplied by the consulted overview pages. |
| Roles and improvement | Review machine operator, assembler, machinist, toolmaker, millwright, planners, supervisors, engineers, inspector, electrician, welder, and apprentice within local role boundaries. Job rotation and cross-training do not establish authorization. Root cause analysis and five whys require evidence; exactly five questions are not mandatory and do not prove a cause. |
| Improvement coordination | Review cause-and-effect diagram, value stream, value stream mapping, workplace organization, waste reduction, improvement suggestion/project, owner and standardization, follow-up review, lessons learned, escalation path, cross-functional team, supplier quality, and customer requirement. A map, proposal, or assigned action does not establish an improvement outcome. |
| Canonical reuse | Preserve shared physical tools/actions, dimension and capacity senses, specification, spare part, asset register, apprenticeship, skills matrix, maintenance terms, stainless steel, and polish. Preserve their existing media/morphology and mark contextual domain review pending. Industrial service, filter, machine verb, and lubricant grease have their separately specified meanings. |
| Morphology | Review explicit boundaries and suffix spelling for casting, forging, coupling, lubrication, calibration, thickness, flatness, concentricity, assembler, and machinist. Review multi + meter, de + burr, change + over, bottle + neck, tool + maker, mill + wright, mis + alignment, in + balance (surface im before b), lock + out, and tag + out for modern transparency. Maintain, service, bearing, filter, overhaul, kanban, and micrometer remain null. |
| Variants and difficulty | Review machining center/centre, trolley/cart, molding/moulding, caliper/calliper forms, aluminum/aluminium, and abbreviations such as BOM and WIP. Variant candidates are not additional senses. Difficulty assignments are editorial candidates, not certified CEFR results. |
| Visuals and audio | Acquire and inspect appropriate sourced production assets, recording original source, creator, license, acquisition state, and available animations. Review object/action distinctions and clarity at gameplay size. Abstract process, uncertainty, acoustic, and statistical senses may remain unsuitable for isolated picture identification. Audio uses ava-sweet: en-US-AvaNeural, rate -15%, pitch +8Hz, volume +0%, no postprocessing. |

## Consulted source scope

Eight source records under `manufacturing-` IDs retain publishers, URLs, consultation dates, and exact scopes. All were consulted on 2026-10-16:

- [BLS Industrial Machinery Mechanics, Machinery Maintenance Workers, and Millwrights](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/installation-maintenance-and-repair/industrial-machinery-mechanics-and-maintenance-workers-and-millwrights.htm): occupational duties, equipment, manuals, diagnosis, precision tools, records, and coordination.
- [BLS Assemblers and Fabricators](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/production/assemblers-and-fabricators.htm): duties, drawings, alignment, assembly, quality, teams, and prototypes.
- [BLS Machinists and Tool and Die Makers](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/production/machinists-and-tool-and-die-makers.htm): duties, machine tools, workpieces, drawings, dimensions, tolerances, and finishing. No feeds, speeds, CNC programs, or setup methods were adopted.
- [BLS Quality Control Inspectors](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/production/quality-control-inspectors.htm): duties, specifications, inspection, measurement, tools, electronic checks, and reporting. No acceptance authority or complete inspection method was established.
- [HSE Maintenance of work equipment](https://www.hse.gov.uk/work-equipment-machinery/maintenance.htm): planning, communication, logs, reliability, competence, and hazard context. Linked procedures were not reviewed; UK requirements were not generalized globally.
- [HSE Inspection of work equipment](https://www.hse.gov.uk/work-equipment-machinery/inspection.htm): scope, risk-based frequency, condition, records, and checklists. Example intervals are not universal schedules.
- [NIST MEP Lean and Process Improvement](https://www.nist.gov/mep/lean-and-process-improvement): page body covering value-stream mapping, workplace organization, changeover, flow, pull signals, and improvement context. Linked guides, cases, training, and statistical methods were not reviewed.
- [OSHA Control of Hazardous Energy overview](https://www.osha.gov/control-hazardous-energy/): energy categories, unexpected startup, stored energy, roles, and training context. Linked standards, fact sheets, and procedures were not reviewed. No shutdown, isolation, verification, lockout, tagout, or restart instructions were adopted.

These pages support contextual relevance, not vocabulary-frequency rankings or term-by-term approval. They do not grant media rights, establish universal qualifications, or certify a safe work method. All term references remain `pending_term_check`.

Record reviewer identity, expertise, date, region, source edition, exact canonical or expression ID, decision, and revised wording before changing a release gate. Language, professional domain, morphology, picture suitability, pronunciation, provenance, packaging, and complete-theme readiness require their own evidence. Source preview, downloaded asset, converted asset, and integrated media must remain distinct states.
