# Cleaning & Facilities expert-review brief

## Status and scope

The complete draft contains 500 distinct canonical senses and 100 expressions: the shared 100/20 Foundation plus 400 specialist memberships and 80 specialist expressions. Each module has 50 senses and 10 expressions. The specialist expansion creates 207 senses and reuses 193. Independent editorial review corrected physical move/push/pull meanings, surface-soiling grease, accessibility and window definitions, data-accuracy reuse, and contextual examples and expressions. This does not constitute qualified language, cleaning, facilities, safety, regional, morphology, or media approval. Every required release gate remains pending; no word image or recording was acquired.

All 400 specialist senses have a scored route in their own module. There are 182 new pending morphology candidates: 153 composites and 29 explicit atomic decisions. The remaining 25 analyses stay null. Null and atomic entries have actual same-module Phrase focuses; atomic exposure is not itself a scored route. Structural reconstruction and inventory completeness do not establish linguistic validity or professional suitability.

The course covers communication about equipment, products, visible conditions, work arrangements, records, building services, assigned responsibilities, and concerns. It does not teach chemical mixing, product dilution, cleaning or disinfection procedures, equipment operation, repair, lifting, electrical work, pest treatment, waste classification, or emergency response. Applicable responsibilities and source limitations remain explicit.

## Review assignments

| Area | Required decisions |
|---|---|
| Spaces and equipment | Verify cupboard versus general housekeeping storage, service lift, restroom, laundry space, floor scrubber, polisher, vacuum cleaner, squeegee, duster, mop components, carts, dispensers, and storage equipment. Clarify local names and whether illustrations unambiguously show the relevant tool rather than its action. |
| Products and surfaces | Review detergent, surface sanitizer, disinfectant, soap, concentrate, ready-to-use products, refills, material categories, and fixtures. Household source definitions are not workplace or healthcare standards. Fragrance means a pleasant smell here, not an added perfume ingredient. Composition and compatibility cannot be established from appearance. |
| Visible conditions | Distinguish dirt, dust, stains, smudges, residue, unwanted grease soiling, limescale, suspected mold, odor, and litter. Cleaning grease is unwanted oily or fatty material that soils a surface; it is separate from an industrial semisolid lubricant. Visual observations do not identify hazards or authorize treatment. |
| Actions and states | Verify the actual physical senses of move, push, and pull, keeping them separate from digital move and version-control push/pull. Keep dust and mop verbs distinct from nouns, refill from refill contents, and dry adjective from the moisture-removal verb. Soak and separate examples assess transitive verbs. Available, accessible, vacant, and occupied communicate different states and do not imply permission or compliance. |
| Scope and product information | Review cleaning frequency, zones, routine/deep/spot/periodic cleaning, contact time, surface compatibility, concentration, dilution, color coding, and cross-contamination. No universal durations, concentrations, methods, or efficacy outcomes are supplied. A spotless or streak-free surface is not proof of disinfection. |
| Waste and linen | Review waste-stream names and local collection acceptance; recyclable does not mean accepted in every local program. Waste transfer note is a UK-context non-hazardous business-waste document; the course supplies no universal duty or retention rule. Hazardous waste, sharps, bodily fluids, and contaminated waste require appropriate qualified classification and handling review. |
| Buildings and maintenance | Confirm building, facility, premises, components, fixtures, utilities, maintenance, preventive/reactive maintenance, repair request, asset, service interval, and records. A window is an opening often fitted with glass rather than necessarily glazed. A repair request concerns corrective work; a broader maintenance request can also concern inspection or preventive attention. |
| Health, hazards, and access | Verify risk categories, skin irritation versus dermatitis, exposure, hazard pictograms, signal words, labels, PPE categories, restricted access, and reported security concerns. Official symbols and any acquired examples need source and licensing review. An illustration cannot certify equipment protection, accessible design, or safe conditions. |
| Records and systems | Reuse the shared data accuracy sense instead of duplicating record accuracy. The stock-count expression compares the quantity held with the recorded stock level, not the goods with a number. UI filter is a result-limiting control, capacity is available work capability, and dependency is a prerequisite task or condition. Preserve distinct records, synchronization states, and evidence without assuming receipt or authorization. |
| Roles and coordination | Housekeeper has broader household or guest-room upkeep responsibilities than a room attendant. Facilities, security, maintenance, grounds, laundry, waste, and pest-control role scopes depend on actual arrangements. Fictional reporting relationships do not establish universal authority. |
| Canonical sharing | Reuse service standard, spare part, estimated time of arrival, work progress report, floor plan, asset register, and handover meeting. The fixed urinal fixture differs from the portable clinical vessel. Drain is a building opening/channel noun rather than the existing liquid-removal verb. Facial tissue differs from biological tissue. |
| Morphology | Review explicit affixes, compounds, word boundaries, and spellings: scrub/scrubb + er, occupy/occupi + ed, facility/faciliti + es, re + fill, un + available, micro + fiber, photo + graph, and ground + s + keep + er. QR remains an abbreviation. Hyphen boundaries are individually authored. Review lexicalized cupboard and intact bases for teaching value. Unknown analyses remain null, including squeegee, detergent, fragrance, porcelain, premises, maintenance, and dermatitis. |
| Variants and difficulty | Review microfiber/microfibre, mold/mould, odor/odour, faucet/tap, worktop/countertop, cart/trolley, lift/elevator, refuse-bin alternatives, and preventive/preventative. Variants do not add counted senses. Difficulty labels are provisional editorial candidates. |
| Pictures and pronunciation | Each new sense has an original contextual brief but picture suitability is false pending review. Smells, condition judgments, material composition, and administrative states may require context and remain unsuitable for isolated picture questions. ## Consulted source limits

Eight sources were consulted on 2026-10-16. Every term reference remains pending_term_check. Their actual scope follows:

- [Janitors and Building Cleaners](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/building-and-grounds-cleaning/janitors-and-building-cleaners.htm) (U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics): Duties and work-context passages consulted for cleaning, equipment, supplies, schedules, repair reporting, and role variation. The overview displays an August 27, 2026 update. No wage, forecast, universal qualification, equipment-use procedure, or term-level approval is adopted.

- [Administrative Services and Facilities Managers](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/management/administrative-services-managers.htm) (U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics): Facilities duties and work-context passages consulted for buildings, grounds, equipment, supplies, staff, records, and maintenance coordination. This occupational overview does not authorize technical work or establish universal management powers.

- [Janitors and Cleaners, Except Maids and Housekeeping Cleaners](https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/37-2011.00) (O*NET OnLine): Tasks and work activities consulted for spaces, supplies, records, equipment, and reporting vocabulary. Listed chemical mixing, pesticide, and repair activities are occupational descriptions, not procedures or authorization adopted by this course.

- [Common risks in cleaning](https://www.hse.gov.uk/cleaning/topics/index.htm) (Health and Safety Executive): Actual category list consulted for risk assessment, slips and trips, dermatitis, musculoskeletal disorders, COSHH, and work at height. The page displays a March 16, 2023 update. UK context only; linked detailed guidance was not read or adopted as operating instructions.

- [When and How to Clean and Disinfect Your Home](https://www.cdc.gov/hygiene/about/when-and-how-to-clean-and-disinfect-your-home.html) (Centers for Disease Control and Prevention): Actual definitions and surface-label/contact-time context consulted on the January 31, 2025 household page. Household context is not a workplace or healthcare standard. Chemical strengths, mixing, sequences, temperatures, durations, and clinical instructions are not adopted.

- [Hazard Communication](https://www.osha.gov/hazcom) (Occupational Safety and Health Administration): Overview consulted for hazard information, labels, safety data sheets, pictograms, and signal words. U.S. context only. Full linked standards, quickcards, legal requirements, and training procedures were not reviewed. No artwork or pictograms were acquired.

- [Dispose of business or commercial waste: Waste transfer notes](https://www.gov.uk/dispose-business-commercial-waste/waste-transfer-notes) (GOV.UK): Actual waste-transfer-note page consulted for the document concept and its non-hazardous commercial-waste context. The old managing-your-waste URL redirects here. Retention periods, form-completion instructions, and regional legal duties are not taught or generalized; linked forms and the full guide were not reviewed.

- [Recycling Basics and Benefits](https://www.epa.gov/recycle/recycling-basics-and-benefits) (U.S. Environmental Protection Agency): Opening definition, collection/processing overview, and recyclable-product clarification consulted, including that local collection programs may accept different materials. The page displays a June 22, 2026 update. No economic figures, universal acceptance rules, linked infographics, or assets were adopted.

These sources support occupational and communication context. They do not certify the full vocabulary, provide a frequency ranking, approve an operating method, establish global legal requirements, or convey asset rights. No source preview is an acquired word image.
