scoped production proposal. The image is **not integrated**. Root independently
viewed the acquired original, the 414 by 512 crop, and all 15 native comparison
states at their captured size.

The tall solid body, paired loop handles and open cavity remain distinct from
the accepted squat slotted shopping basket. The five Match pair colors and
numbered badges preserve those cues, as do Memory's full-opacity matched and

generic bag can share a form with a gift bag; this source has no gift-specific
decoration. The decision is AI visual/editorial review, not participant testing.

exact decoded pixels; preserved both images with only their lease active; and
