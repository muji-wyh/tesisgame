Tiny Treats - Bakery Interior set

This cozy, stylized low-poly asset pack has everything you need to build a warm and welcoming bakery interior. It features a mix of countertops in different styles and sizes, along with must-have equipment like a coffee machine, stand mixer, scale, and cash register. The set also includes a variety of sweet treats, baking ingredients,  floors/walls/doors, and plenty of decorative assets to bring your bakery to life.

This set pairs great with my Baked goods and Bakery Building sets. Together, they let you create a cozy bakery  for your game!



  • 58+ Stylised low-poly 3D models, perfect for games across all platforms, including mobile.
  • Textured using a single gradient atlas texture (1024x1024) that can be downsampled up to 128x128 for further optimization.
  • Models are included as .OBJ, .FBX and .GLTF, That means the files work in most 3D game engines (including Unity, Godot, Unreal Engine, Roblox, and more).
  • Free for personal and commercial use, no attribution required. (CC0 Licensed)
