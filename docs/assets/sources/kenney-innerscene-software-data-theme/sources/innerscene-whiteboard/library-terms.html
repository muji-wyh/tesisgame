Free Parts Library Terms

The Free Parts Library holds 3D models, CAD parts and 2D drawings contributed by people using the Innerscene 3D Viewer, plus parts we add from public-domain collections. Contributions are released under the CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain Dedication). Parts added from other collections carry their source’s status, shown on each part page: either CC0 or public domain as a work of the US federal government (17 U.S.C. §105), which applies in the United States and may differ elsewhere.

How long parts are kept

Library files are kept for a limited time from submission, depending on their size:

  • Up to 5 MB: 5 years
  • 5 MB to 15 MB: 4 years
  • 15 MB to 30 MB: 3 years
  • 30 MB to 60 MB: 2 years
  • Over 60 MB: 1 year

Ordinary share links that are not in the library are kept for 90 days.

Review and removal

Submissions are reviewed before they appear. Innerscene Corp may decline, edit the description of, or remove any part at any time. If you believe a part infringes your rights, use Report on the part’s viewer page or follow our copyright policy.
