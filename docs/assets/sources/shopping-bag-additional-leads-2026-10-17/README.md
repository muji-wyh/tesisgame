## Visual and semantic recommendation

The source has a broad open cavity, two separate loop handles, a tall solid-sided
body, smooth warm shading and no writing or branding. It is friendly and clean at
small size. Its shiny handles and smooth body are more rounded than Kenney's
faceted props, but its isolated three-quarter presentation and clear volume form
a plausible neighboring style. This is an editorial assessment, not a user study.

The existing brief in `data/professional/lexemes/retail-pilot.json` asks for a
reusable fabric bag, flexible sides and purchased items peeking out. This source
does not demonstrate fabric or flexibility and is empty. Do not claim that it
meets that exact brief.

The approved definition is broader: "A bag used to carry purchased items."
Fabric and visible contents are depiction choices rather than necessary parts
of that definition. An empty plain retail carrier can support that sense without
adding unapproved answers or changing the definition. A separate, explicitly
recorded brief revision is reasonable to consider:

> Show a plain open retail shopping bag in a three-quarter view, with a tall
> solid-sided body, two separate loop handles and a clearly visible opening.
> Keep the body and handles distinct from the low, slotted shopping basket.
> Exclude wheels, backpack straps, a closed top, logos, ribbons, gift tags and
> tissue that makes the picture specifically a gift bag. The bag may be empty
> so that its opening and handles remain clear at minimum card size.

This recommendation does not narrow the sense to paper bags or assert a
specific material. Shopping bags and gift bags can share a shape; a picture of
an empty carrier cannot establish intended use absolutely. The absence of a
bow, gift tag, tissue or decorative motif avoids making a gift-specific reading
dominant. Native comparison and independent acceptance remain required.

## Small-size evidence

`size-study/source-size-comparison.png` is a **Pillow source-image simulation**,
with the original PNG, a 414 x 512 study crop and the exact accepted basket PNG.
It uses image areas 102 x 52 (Match) and 120 x 54 (Memory), at alpha 1.00 and
0.68. The original and cropped bag remain visibly open; cropping makes the
paired handles and cavity easier to inspect. The basket remains squat, slotted
and broad-handled, so the distinction does not rely only on red versus green.

`source-size-comparison-3x.png` is a nearest-neighbor enlargement for pixel
inspection. `measurements.json` records exact dimensions, hashes and processing.
The first comparison was inspected at its 710 x 340 image size, then its
enlargement was inspected. Both silver handles are thinner in the faded state,
but remain separate from the tall body and dark opening.

No actual WordCard was instantiated. The simulation omits badge, border,
pair-specific tint, runtime sampling and motion. It does not establish actual
matched-card occlusion, production loading, cache release, course readiness or
participant recognition. No Godot/native renderer, test or build was run.
