# Retail theme source record

Four exact PNG sidecars, selected original sources, the shared texture and original license are integrated. Theme status remains pending; ambient motion is ready after bounded metadata and production raw-loader verification.

The original Mini Market 1.0 models come from [Kenney](https://kenney.nl/assets/mini-market), with additional credits to Fleur Keijsers and Guus Vermeulen, under CC0 1.0. They were previously acquired in the user's Kenney All-in-1 3.7.0 library. The original license, selected GLBs and required shared texture are mapped with hashes in file-destinations.json. Original acquisition inventory is retained unchanged alongside a destination-aware provenance record.

The employee's original idle is the only delivered clip. Its torso, arms and head rotate, with no source root translation. Sixteen samples play over four seconds at 4 FPS. Frame zero is the reduced-motion rest; static front/side T-poses are inspection evidence only. No procedural joint animation, mesh replacement or material change was introduced. The other 31 employee clips and two door models were inventoried only.

Review covers source anatomy/expression, all sampled poses, exact static/motion RGB composition, native-sized images, recorded live native timing, reduced-motion rest, hidden pause and cleanup. It does not claim continuous visual playback, audio listening, microphone recognition, curriculum review or occupational certification. The root reviewer independently accepted the exact source/frame/pose evidence. Asset reviews now record that limited scope; no additional review claim is implied.
