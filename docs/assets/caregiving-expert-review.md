# Home & Elder Care expert-review brief

## Scope and status

The Caregiving course contains a complete **draft inventory of 500 distinct canonical senses and 100 expressions**. Its two shared foundation modules contribute 100 senses and 20 expressions. Modules03 through module10 contribute 400 specialist memberships and 80 original expressions: 183 new senses and 217 reused canonical senses. Every module remains draft. All specialist entries and expressions require recorded care-domain review, in addition to language review. No reviewer approval is claimed.

This is English communication practice, not a care qualification, clinical protocol, equipment-use guide, or legal advice. The draft does not teach medicine doses, diagnostic tests, transfer or restraint techniques, emergency procedures, individual diets, fluid targets, or funding entitlements. Recognizing an item or action does not authorize a learner to use it or perform it. The expression about sudden confusion retains the need for urgent medical assessment without supplying a diagnostic test or country-specific response number.

All 183 new word illustrations are pending sense-specific briefs with picture suitability false. All new word and expression recordings remain pending and retain the approved Ava preset. There are 156 new pending morphology graphs and 27 deliberately unauthored analyses. A structurally valid graph does not establish linguistic correctness.

## Canonical reuse and open exercise work

The 217 reused senses retain their existing identity and on-demand source file. Shared refrigerator, fold, hang, portion, ingredient, and decline senses are not copied into new entries. The definition of decline was broadened to include an offer alongside a request or transaction; the prior Retail example remains compatible. Clothing-related dress differs from applying a wound covering; human memory differs from computer storage; a food menu differs from an interface menu. Those have separate definitions and canonical IDs.

The new words retain only the practice capabilities supported by actual draft content. A phrase capability requires an authored expression that assesses the sense in one of its modules; unsupported phrase claims were removed. All entries still require a reachable scored exercise before release. Atomic collection is exposure only. Image suitability, reviewed composition, and contextual assessment are separate editorial decisions.

## Required sign-off record

Record the exact canonical or expression ID, reviewer role, jurisdiction, source edition/date, review date, decision, revised wording, and unresolved concerns. Language, domain, morphology, pronunciation, visual suitability, asset provenance, and delivery are independent gates. Accepted regional forms do not create extra vocabulary entries or imply interchangeable professional credentials.

The 12 new context sources were consulted on 2026-10-16 and are registered in sources.json. All term references remain pending_term_check. All new media stays pending. No source consultation, count assertion, or successful structural test may be used to mark a course, module, or entry ready.
