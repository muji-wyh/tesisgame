# Restaurants & Food Service expert-review brief

## Scope and status

The Food Service course has a complete **draft inventory of 500 distinct canonical senses and 100 expressions**. Its two shared foundation modules contribute 100 senses and 20 expressions. Modules03 through module10 contribute 400 specialist memberships and 80 original expressions: 171 new senses and 229 reused canonical senses. All modules remain draft. Every specialist entry and expression requires recorded food-domain review in addition to language review, including entries outside the dedicated safety module. A `not_required` label cannot bypass that requirement.

This is workplace English communication practice. The draft names equipment, ingredients, service roles, records, and preparation actions without supplying temperatures, holding times, chemical concentrations, recipes, cooking procedures, knife techniques, safe-food decisions, or emergency steps. An action definition does not authorize a learner to perform the action. Examples ask for current information, approved workplace instructions, or qualified clarification. Ingredient and allergen questions do not promise that a food is free of an allergen.

All 171 new word images are pending sense-specific briefs with picture suitability false. All new word and expression recordings remain pending with the approved Ava preset. There are 141 explicit pending morphology graphs and 30 deliberately unauthored analyses. No source consultation or structural validation counts as language, safety, or production-asset approval.

## Canonical reuse and exercise coverage

The 229 reused senses retain their canonical identity and original on-demand source file. Shared kitchen, refrigerator, ingredient, portion, food label, payment, inventory, and record vocabulary is not duplicated. Genuine distinctions remain separate: culinary salt versus a cryptographic salt, a food-service server versus a computer server, a serving plate versus the verb plate, a cook versus cooking, and worker versus appliance dishwasher. The return-phone-call sense of callback is reused; the programming function sense is not.

Every current phrase capability is backed by an authored expression that assesses the sense in one of its modules. Basic atomic terms still need a reviewed scored route; collection alone provides exposure. The pending image brief does not grant a picture practice mode, and an unreviewed compositional graph does not make an exercise ready. These open routes remain visible in the release audit.
