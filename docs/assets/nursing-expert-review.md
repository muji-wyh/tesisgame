# Nursing curriculum: expert review queue

Curriculum revision: professional-v1. Review status: pending. This is a review brief for the 500-sense, 100-expression Nursing draft, not approval to use it as clinical training. The 100 shared foundation terms are generic workplace English; the 400 specialist memberships require clinical review. All 80 specialist expressions require clinical review as well. No new word image or pronunciation asset has been approved.

The definitions and examples are original language-learning drafts. They ask learners to describe experiences, clarify information, locate approved guidance, and communicate responsibilities. They contain no medicine doses, diagnostic thresholds, treatment selection rules, emergency techniques, or procedural sequences. This scope still needs expert confirmation at the individual-entry level.

## Shared canonical senses

Nursing modules04 through module10 reuse 28 existing senses. Review their Nursing use while preserving cross-course identity unless a genuinely different meaning is established. The draft definitions of measure.find-dimensions.v, lift.raise-item.v, and observation.recorded-measurement-or-case.n were broadened to reflect the shared meaning; earlier Retail and Software examples must still fit.

Particular checks:

- near-miss.narrowly-avoided-incident.n: reporting-framework differences need an explicit decision.
- delegation.task-authority-assignment.n: the generic assignment meaning is reused; clinical accountability and scope must be explained by qualified review rather than inferred from the word alone.
- contamination.unwanted-substance-presence.n: verify the general meaning covers the intended clinical examples without implying that every contaminant is a pathogen.
- confidentiality.authorized-information-disclosure.n and data-privacy.personal-information-protection.n: retain the distinction between restricted disclosure and broader handling of personal information.
- owner.task-responsible-person.n: review whether the local team prefers responsible person; its older atomic morphology candidate also needs correction or explicit justification before release.

## Evidence required for sign-off

For each reviewed canonical ID and expression ID, retain the reviewer role, jurisdiction, source edition/date, review date, decision (approve, revise, or reject), exact revised wording, and unresolved issues. A language reviewer and the required clinical reviewer must both sign off. Morphology, exercise reachability, visual suitability, pronunciation, and asset provenance are independent gates.

Accepted forms also need review. physical therapist, speech and language therapist, speech-language pathologist, carer, dietician, mobilise, diarrhoea, specimen labelling, EHR, and MAR are draft alternatives. Their presence does not establish interchangeable credentials, pronunciation, or morphology. Abbreviations and spelling alternatives count as one canonical sense and must not inflate inventory totals.

The pending-source references in the JSON remain pending_term_check until their individual meanings and uses have actually been checked. Reading an authoritative overview does not approve every term linked to it. All word images and recordings remain pending, and the full Nursing theme is pending. No module may be marked ready until the content, media, theme, delivery, and scored-exercise gates pass.
