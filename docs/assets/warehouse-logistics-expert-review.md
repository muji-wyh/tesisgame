# Warehouse and Logistics expert-review brief

## Scope and status

The Warehouse and Logistics course has a complete **draft inventory of 500 distinct canonical senses and 100 expressions**. The shared foundation contributes 100 senses and 20 expressions. Modules03 through module10 add 400 specialist memberships and 80 expressions: 149 new senses and 251 reused canonical senses. All modules remain draft. Every specialist entry and expression requires recorded domain review in addition to language review, including content outside module08. A `not_required` label cannot bypass this gate.

The content supports recognition, workplace communication, records comparison, and requests for current information. It does not teach forklift operation, lifting technique, load calculation, loading or docking sequences, machine repair, energy isolation, emergency action, temperature limits, route dimensions, transport law, tariffs, or inventory-valuation methods. Naming a task or recognizing equipment does not authorize a learner to perform the activity.

All 149 new sense-specific picture briefs retain picture suitability false and pending media status. Word and expression audio retain the approved Ava preset and remain pending. There are 136 explicit pending morphology graphs and 13 unauthored analyses. All new senses have an authored scored route through a relevant expression or a multi-part graph. This coverage does not make unreviewed exercises playable.

## Review assignments

| Reviewer | Terms and expressions | Required decisions and evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Warehouse educator, equipment specialist, and regional language reviewer | module03; forklift, reach truck, handheld terminal, loading dock, dock leveler, dock plate, dock seal, pallet rack, rack beam, rack upright, rack guard | Confirm equipment identity, scope, and regional usage. Dock leveler/leveller and forklift/forklift truck are accepted forms, not extra counted senses. Loading bay and loading dock must remain distinguishable in their context. Pallet jack is reused rather than adding pallet truck as a new inventory item. Static art must not imply equipment authorization, complete impact protection, or a safe vehicle/building gap. |
| Machinery and facilities specialists | module03; charging station, wrapping machine, strapping machine, pallet scale, mezzanine, pedestrian walkway | Check visual recognition and local terminology. Show inactive equipment without exposed moving mechanisms, battery connections, operating demonstrations, or access instructions. A site contact must identify the actual pedestrian routes and authorized mezzanine access. A generic image cannot establish a safe route, setup, load limit, or approved charging arrangement. |
| Packaging educator and goods-handling specialist | module04; package, label, crate, drum, sack, stretch wrap, shrink wrap, corner protector, void fill, gross weight, tare weight | Verify containers, material properties, and meaningful distinctions. Package is the physical-goods noun rather than the software-package noun; label is an information-marker noun distinct from the labeling verb. Gross weight depends on the specified measurement basis; tare describes the excluded container or packaging. Do not infer drum contents from its shape or teach heat-treatment, fastening, restraint, or cushioning procedures. |
| Warehouse-process and quality-control reviewers | module05; receive, pick, stow, store, consolidate, deconsolidate, palletize, depalletize, secure, seal, relabel, repack, segregate, quarantine, allocate, recount, reweigh, release | Confirm ordinary verb senses and natural examples. Seal is a closing action distinct from the existing closure noun; release removes a stock hold under authority and differs from a software version. Separate planned allocation, physical movement, controlled hold, and authorized release. Confirm that the reused document verb is suitable outside care records. Repacking, relabeling, or release may affect traceability and must not be treated as automatic worker permissions. |
| Shipping educator and transport-document specialist | module06; customer order, order line, pick list, pick quantity, picked quantity, advance shipping notice, shipment, outbound order, inbound delivery | Check the planned versus actual quantity distinction. Shipment is drafted as the transport movement, while the existing consignment refers to the goods group; determine whether that distinction is natural and useful for the launch regions. Inbound delivery is an arrival event, while incoming shipment describes the incoming goods. Advance shipping notice is information before arrival, not proof of receipt. |
| Logistics and legal-domain reviewers | module06; split shipment, partial shipment, carrier, courier, freight forwarder, consignor, consignee, proof of delivery, bill of lading, shipping manifest | Verify the different transport roles and document functions. Split shipment describes an order divided across movements; partial shipment is a dispatch that leaves part of the order unshipped, so one event may fit both descriptions. A named consignee is not automatically the owner or legally entitled recipient in every circumstance. Proof of delivery concerns a recorded event and does not automatically prove condition, acceptance, or a particular person's receipt. A bill of lading's legal effects vary with document form and jurisdiction; the language definition cannot establish them. Do not copy real seals, signatures, personal addresses, or official transport documents. |
| Delivery-operations and planning reviewers | module06; delivery slot, collection window, cutoff time, delivery exception, failed delivery, return shipment, order fulfillment, cross-docking | Verify provider and site conventions. Estimates and bookings are not universal guarantees. An exception does not identify its cause or dictate a redelivery/refund decision. Returns require the actual applicable arrangement. Cross-docking is a recognition concept rather than a vehicle movement or goods-handling procedure. |
| Inventory-control specialist and software-domain reviewer | module07; quantity, in-transit stock, bin location, location code, perpetual inventory, available stock, allocated stock, stock reservation, inventory freeze, recount request, blind count | Confirm the quantity's unit and the status definitions used by the actual system. Perpetual inventory records transactions continuously through the process and does not guarantee real-time accuracy. Book stock was excluded because it duplicated the existing stock-level sense. Allocation and reservation can overlap in software; retain both only if the reviewed curriculum gives a useful distinction. A freeze applies to specified activities, not necessarily all movement. Blind count hides the expected quantity from the counter; no procedure is prescribed. |
| Inventory planning, product-quality, and finance reviewers | module07; stockout, replenishment task, replenishment quantity, minimum stock, maximum stock, safety stock, inventory accuracy, first expired, first out, shelf life, stock aging, inventory valuation | Review planning terms and measure definitions without introducing universal limits or formulas. Maximum stock is a planning target, not a rack or vehicle load rating. Safety stock is a planning buffer, not a safety guarantee. First expired, first out selects eligible goods by earliest expiration and never implies that expired goods are usable. Shelf life depends on the specified product conditions and requirements. Stock-aging dates and valuation policies vary; the lessons do not authorize disposal or accounting decisions. |
| Qualified site-safety, machinery, and privacy reviewers | module08; machine guard, emergency stop, vehicle lane, exclusion zone, load stability, damaged racking, equipment authorization, isolation procedure | Check recognition and escalation language across every relevant module. An emergency stop does not itself establish hazardous-energy isolation. Isolation belongs to the specified process and qualified authorized people; no lockout or shutdown sequence is taught. A visitor badge does not establish operating authority. Current local arrangements govern access and reporting. Temperature, PPE, materials, and cold-chain questions stay with the responsible specialist. Restrict personal-data examples to authorized purpose and minimum necessary information. |
| Warehouse systems educator and data-control reviewer | module09; warehouse management system, transport management system, barcode symbology, scan event, scan failure, duplicate scan, location scan, item master, unit of measure, goods receipt, goods issue, movement record, stock status, audit count, system discrepancy | Verify meanings across software products. Barcode symbology is an encoding system; scanning does not by itself validate the underlying item, destination, or authorization. Duplicate scan is a suspected repeated event requiring review, not permission to delete data. A unit must be compatible before amounts are compared. Goods receipt, goods issue, movement, and stock status describe different record dimensions. Use original fictional interfaces and nonfunctional identifiers; no password may be shared through a routine message. |
| Warehouse workforce and regional role reviewers | module10; warehouse operative, order picker, packer, receiving clerk, shipping clerk, inventory controller, forklift operator, warehouse supervisor, dispatch coordinator, transport planner, shift briefing, handover checklist, equipment defect report, delivery performance, order accuracy | Check titles, assigned responsibilities, and regional usage. Roles can overlap and a title does not establish qualification or decision authority. Handovers should identify outstanding work and the responsible contact. Metric denominators, event definitions, and reporting periods must be stated before comparisons. Defect reports communicate observations and do not substitute for inspection or repair. |
| English morphologist | all 136 graphs and 13 null analyses | Check genuine constituents, semantic transparency, lexicalization, irregular forms, and deeper analysis of intact stems. Carrier preserves carry with surface carri before er; wrapping, strapping, shipping, controller, and planner preserve doubled consonants. Depalletize contains de + pallet + ize. Cross-docking and in-transit stock preserve hyphens. Warehouse management system preserves ordered boundaries and manage + ment. Goods, shipment, and other lexicalized formations need semantic review. Occupational source pages are not evidence for morphological sign-off. |
| Art director, language educator, and accessibility reviewer | all 149 new picture briefs | Acquire and inspect production artwork with provenance and gameplay-size review. Show friendly, inclusive workers and realistic equipment recognition. Keep machinery inactive, traffic and pedestrian contexts clear, and people away from hazardous mechanisms or loading gaps. Do not invent credentials, safety certification, site layouts, measurable limits, real addresses, signatures, or functional barcodes. Abstract process and record concepts may need context scenes and must not automatically enter picture-identification practice. |
