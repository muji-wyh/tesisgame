# Barcode picture

The integrated barcode picture is a sourced linear code on an unbranded parcel, framed close enough to keep separate bars and spaces visible at the minimum Match and Memory card sizes. It is an illustration; no valid machine-readable encoding, real product identity or price is claimed.

The barcode artwork is by netalloy, acquired from [ClipSafari](https://www.clipsafari.com/clips/o38797-barcode) with its CC0 attribution and the CC0 deed retained. The original [Openclipart page](https://openclipart.org/detail/38797/netalloy-barcode) was unavailable and is not claimed downloaded. The complete parcel is from [Kenney Car Kit 3.1](https://kenney.nl/assets/car-kit), CC0, acquired through the existing All-in-1 library. Original SVG, GLB, external texture, previews, licenses and acquisition records are retained in [the source record](sources/professional-retail-barcode/source-record.json).

Only the source SVG viewBox changes, from `0 0 300 300` to `92 79 173 88`; the original bars and spaces remain intact. The crop is rasterized at 692 by 352 and displayed on a planar artwork surface over the unchanged complete parcel. Camera and lighting create the package-edge close-up. The 768 by 440 render is proportionally reduced to 512 by 293. No animation is supplied.
