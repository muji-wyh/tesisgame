# Hospitality and Tourism expert-review brief

## Scope and status

The course has a complete **draft inventory of 500 distinct canonical senses and 100 expressions**. The shared foundation contributes 100 senses and 20 expressions. Modules03 through module10 contribute 400 specialist memberships and 80 expressions: 177 new senses and 223 reused canonical senses. Every module remains draft. All specialist entries and expressions require recorded domain review in addition to language review, including content outside module08. A `not_required` label cannot bypass this requirement.

The course supports a friendly welcome, accurate information, individual guest preferences, privacy, and clear coordination. It does not teach cleaning methods, chemical handling, lifting, appliance operation, repair, guiding techniques, emergency actions, tax or financial calculations, visa eligibility, or universal legal entitlements. Recognizing a task or role does not establish permission to carry it out.

All 177 new sense-specific picture briefs retain pending media status and picture suitability false. Word and expression audio retain the approved Ava preset and remain pending. The course adds 144 explicit pending morphology graphs and 33 unauthored analyses. Every new sense has an authored scored route through a relevant expression or a multi-part graph. Neither this coverage nor the separately integrated Home preview makes unreviewed content playable.
