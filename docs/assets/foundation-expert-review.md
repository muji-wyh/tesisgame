# Shared Foundation expert-review brief

## Scope and status

This repair updates the shared two-module Foundation inventory used by all 20 professional courses. It preserves 100 canonical senses, 20 expressions, all canonical IDs, and existing course memberships. All 100 senses have an authored scored exercise route after the expression repair. Content, morphology, and word media remain pending; no module is ready for release.

The change adds 24 pending morphology decisions. Fifteen propose explicit compositions; nine propose intact modern teaching units. The 76 existing analyses are unchanged and are not endorsed by this repair. All 100 decisions require qualified linguistic review before learners are shown a reviewed morphological explanation.

## Expression and editorial review

Nine atomic senses and five senses with formerly missing morphology had no scored exercise. The existing ten expressions per module are rebalanced to assess these senses in context, preserving sense and part of speech. A separate peer inspected focus meanings, naturalness, and removed focuses. The final wording incorporates its shift-break, proposed-solution, and safe-route refinements. Removed focuses retain an authored picture or multi-part morphology route; that structural route is not a claim of available media or review completion.

The following distinctions need to survive future editing:

- A helpful-person contact is distinct from the verb contact. A message, question, and answer are nouns in their assessment expressions.
- An internal phone extension is assessed in a voicemail context. Permission means authorized consent, not general possibility.
- Repeat in this inventory means saying something again. Priority expresses relative importance or urgency. Complete covers finishing an activity or providing everything required; a form is one pedagogical example.
- Collect means gathering or picking items up, without an invented purpose restriction. The example picks documents up from reception.
- A supplier is a commercial provider, distinct from a courier. Attendance is presence, which a record may document. A signature brief shows the resulting written name.
- Any time-zone artwork needs named places or zones, an explicit date, and verified offsets. A generic unexplained clock difference is insufficient.

## Evidence and limits

On 16 October 2026, selected Merriam-Webster headword, meaning, related-form, and/or word-history passages were consulted for all 24 words. Its e-, -or, -ity, and -ure entries were also consulted. Selected Dictionary.com origin passages were consulted for visitor, update, extension, permission, responsibility, clarification, verify, postpone, reject, and solution. The 38 unique used URLs are recorded on the candidate analyses and in the source registry under foundation-mw-* and foundation-dictionary-* IDs.

The word-history evidence can support a relationship while leaving its suitability for this curriculum unresolved. Neither publisher's syllable dots are morphology boundaries. A matching past-participle passage for a separate -en entry was not established, so no such source is cited; broken's headword and history establish the participial relationship while its precise teaching analysis remains pending.

Definitions, examples, and image briefs are original drafts. No dictionary artwork, pronunciation recording, or other distributable asset was acquired. A consulted reference is not a language, domain, regional, morphology, picture-suitability, or license approval.

## Release gates and review sequence

1. A qualified language reviewer checks all 20 expressions and the edited definitions against the canonical senses and appropriate learner levels.
2. A qualified morphology reviewer approves, revises, or rejects the proposed compositions and intact-unit decisions, then separately reviews the older 76 candidates. Reviewer identity, date, exact source scope, and rationale must be recorded.
3. A domain reviewer checks workplace examples, especially authorization, safety reporting, and escalation. Examples communicate about an assigned process and do not replace workplace procedures.
4. An art reviewer acquires and verifies appropriate production word images, including discriminating scenes for abstract senses; source, creator, license, acquisition, and picture suitability must be recorded. Audio uses the approved Ava preset and requires review.
5. The strict content audit and runtime/media tests must pass before any module becomes selectable for scored practice. An authored route with pending material does not satisfy the release gate.

Scratch validation before persistence covered inventory preservation, removal of all 14 Foundation route gaps without new gaps, unchanged old analyses/reviews/media, and absence of production writes during the freeze. Four production regression tests now cover scored routes, exact expression token offsets, same-module focuses, morphology surfaces and distinctions, consulted reference links, editorial meanings, and pending review/media gates. These checks do not substitute for the expert reviews above.
