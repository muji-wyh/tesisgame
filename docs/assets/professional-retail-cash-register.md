## Source and composition

[Tiny Treats Bakery Interior](https://tinytreats.itch.io/bakery-interior), version 1.1 FREE, supplies the original cash register. Isa Lousberg is the creator and Tiny Treats is the publisher. The official source page and original `License.txt` identify CC0 1.0. The free 7,734,063-byte archive was acquired through the official download without an account or payment; the paid Blender source upgrade was not acquired. Original source, license, source-page capture and the inventory of all 719 archive files remain under `docs/assets/sources/professional-retail-cash-register/`.

The unchanged glTF contains two meshes, 1,643 vertices, 1,612 triangles and the original gradient atlas. The handle retains its original child transform. There are no skins or animation clips. The delivered asset is a static PNG; no keys, sale digits, drawer, handle or animation were added.

The accepted still uses the reviewed low-right view and original inspection lighting. A transparent 768-square render was cropped to its alpha bounds with 16-pixel padding and proportionally resized with Lanczos to 512 by 615 pixels. The PNG is 172,442 encoded bytes and 2,519,040 conservatively accounted decoded CPU plus GPU bytes. Its SHA256 is `f3d4af9b28662c135481459cd0eb9f67ef7e30db3633a745a79ce550c00af262`.

The official source preview, source acquisition, candidate acceptance and production integration have separate evidence. Historical pending labels in source receipts remain unchanged.
