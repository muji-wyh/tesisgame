## Collect: resolve identity before clearing the hold

The current ID is gathering, while the definition combines gathering and pickup; its example, image brief and focus expression assess reception pickup. Fresh Oxford and Collins both separate these senses. A level label alone cannot fix this.

The preferred research option is to keep `collect.gather.v` and consistently teach gathering. It has direct selected-sense support: Oxford A2, Collins B1. Proposed editorial difficulty B1 is supported by Collins rather than inferred from CEFR-J's broad A1 row.

Proposed original wording:

- Definition: "To bring items from several people or places into one group."
- Example: "Please collect one completed form from each team."
- Pending picture brief: "A friendly worker receiving fictional completed forms from three different teams and placing them together in one folder; clearly show several sources and one gathered set, without private information."
- Editorial note: "Selected sense: gathering items from several people or places. Oxford labels this sense A2 and Collins B1; B1 is the proposed teaching placement. Reception pickup, hobby collecting and collecting payments are excluded. Image, morphology and expression acceptance remain separate."
- Dependent expression candidate for `expr.foundation.module02.08`: "Could you assist me while I collect one form from each team and return the key?"

The expression is **not approved or retokenized** in this packet. It keeps assist and return conceptually but needs full sentence/meaning/context/token review, including the accepted historical decisions for those neighbors. The existing reception-pickup expression must stay held against a gathering lexeme until that dependent work is accepted. Its current image brief must not be mistaken for a reviewed gathering picture.

Alternative: intentionally teach pickup under a distinct new identity, such as the unreserved proposal `collect.pick-up-items.v`, with an explicit regional usage note. Oxford gives pickup B1, while Collins gives A2 and a British label with an American pick-up preference. This route requires a new canonical identity, migration review of 20 module02 memberships and the expression focus, matching morphology/media identity fields, and progress/requirements-revision decisions. It must not silently reinterpret the old ID or transfer gathering mastery to pickup. No choice or migration has been accepted here.
