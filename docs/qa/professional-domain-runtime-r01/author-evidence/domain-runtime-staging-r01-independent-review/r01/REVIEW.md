## Concrete blockers before runtime acceptance

These are integration gaps present outside the staged three-file seam, not newly discovered defects in that seam.

1. **The live loader still bypasses the owner.** Production `scripts/professional_round_loader.gd:30` calls `catalog.resolve` at line 33, then requests media at 41. Claim at 55-63 checks only selection and pending scope. The integration must create one owner, bind owner/request/token/context/media generation and pending scope, and recheck currency after `theme_selected`, before acquire/prepared and at claim. Otherwise installing the candidate does not activate the audited policy boundary.

2. **Owner retirement has no Play/session cancellation route.** Production `scripts/professional_play.gd:164` starts `session.open_round` at 171; `_round_opened` at 189-202 can publish ready based on the existing source/selection state. The staged owner merely emits retired. An integration that only cancels loader media leaves an already pending storage open eligible to finish. Coordinate Play cancellation and `scripts/professional_session.gd:150` so open jobs become obsolete, while accepted event/wallet/ack jobs remain recoverable and continue under their existing rules.

3. **The final claim still follows destructive view teardown.** `scripts/game_ui.gd:1616` starts stopping/clearing the old presentation, removes cards at 1653-1657, then calls `professional.claim` at 1659. A new token-currency rejection can therefore arrive after the old display is gone. Add claim eligibility immediately before teardown and define/test the outcome of reentrant invalidation during it, while preserving the old-media lease until old view/audio references have been dropped.

4. **Actual application disposal remains unproven.** The owner retires itself, but production Store retains host callbacks/timers in `scripts/professional_store.gd:45-106` without a terminal closed lifecycle; Play's `_exit_tree` at 226 releases its active media lease, and `game_ui._exit_tree` at 4552 only clears selected visual/debug state. This review does not establish a concrete new late-callback bug, but the combined disposal path must explicitly address callbacks, pending open jobs, claimed leases and recoverable settlement state. Do not treat owner retirement as proof of whole-scene safety.
