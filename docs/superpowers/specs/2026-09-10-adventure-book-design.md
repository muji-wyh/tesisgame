# Pip's adventure book

The user authorized planning followed directly by implementation, commit, merge to main, push and deployment. Continue the existing Godot game and its original illustrated vocabulary.

## Experience

Give the learner a deliberate destination for the next five-word lesson. An Explore button in Learn and New adventure in results open Pip's illustrated adventure book. All 12 topics are immediately available, with two representative word pictures, a topic name and an honest New place / Visited / Pip is here label. Suggest an unvisited destination, or the least recently visited after all have been visited. Surprise me retains random selection.

Selecting a destination starts Learn. Revisiting samples fresh words where the topic has enough distinct, nonconfusable vocabulary; Repeat lesson retains the exact five words. Switching quiz modes keeps that lesson. Back preserves the current attempt, lesson position and feedback. The existing collection modal supplies input isolation, touch scrolling and keyboard/controller focus; its reward content and book content are alternate views.

## Continuity

Remember visits only when a lesson successfully starts in Learn. Visits are exposure, never mastery, completion or a second reward currency. Store up to 12 unique known topic IDs, most recent first, in an optional journey section of the existing personal-choices record. Persist an explicitly selected reward world so exploration can continue toward the same next gift. All personal-choice writes preserve toys, backdrop, favorite and journey; medal bytes remain independent.

Failed saves preserve confirmed history, allow play and offer retry in the book. Invalid saves are preserved until they can be loaded. Pending earned medal saves still prevent discarding a round. No timers, daily grind, locked topics or extra assets are required for this improvement.

## Validation and delivery

Check every topic, deterministic seeds, nonconfusable words, invalid selection, revisits, repeat identity, migration, write failures and interleaved room/journey writes. Check modal cancel/pause, pending reward protection, narrow screens, scroll gestures, focus and real browser persistence. Run native/Node checks, export once, browser-test the fixed export and obtain an independent review. Commit and merge to main, push and deploy that export, then verify production hash and core flows.
