Find three matching word and picture pairs. Two cards have no match. Three mistakes end the round. Each round allows one hint, shared by touch, keyboard, and controller. Use touch, Tab and Enter, or an Xbox controller. Use the D-pad or left stick to move, A to choose or hold the chest, B to go back, X for a hint, LB and RB to change seasons, and Y or Menu for My rewards. Hint marks a matching pair with stars and speaks its word, without a penalty. The hint button shows Used afterward. Only a new round restores the hint. Where supported, press Voice to start listening immediately and press Voice again to stop. Browser speech may process audio remotely; the game saves no voice or transcripts. Say an English word or a sentence such as “I see a doll”. Live words appear as you speak; only completed speech can match cards. You can still tap cards. Four season buttons change the theme and keep your choice for replays. New rounds prefer fresh words. My rewards shows six medals per season, with three pieces per medal. Swipe to explore My rewards. Motion follows your device's reduced-motion setting.
