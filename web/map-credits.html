Pip and Words

The art of Pip and Words

Our adventure atlas uses the following acquired artwork. Thank you to its creators.

Friendly faces and game library

Unmodified Twemoji 14.0.2 illustrations by Twitter, Inc. and other contributors, licensed under CC BY 4.0, appear in player avatars, game choices, and the Voice Pop welcome screen.

Typography

Nunito by the Nunito Project Authors, licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

Dimensional island destinations

Kenney / Kenney Vleugels: Fantasy Town Kit 2.0, Nature Kit, and Castle Kit. Dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0. Original downloaded models were assembled, lit, and rendered in Blender to create fourteen island dioramas and the map's scenery layers.

Panoramic sky

Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy by Greg Zaal / Poly Haven, dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0. A sky-only crop of the downloaded environment image forms the map background.

Foreground clouds

Background Elements by Kenney, dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0. The original transparent cloud artwork supplies the map's foreground cloud layer.

These source models and illustrations are static. The game adds scrolling, parallax, floating flags, and interface motion separately; the source assets do not supply animated clips.

Earlier atlas and shared interface sources

The earlier atlas artwork remains credited below. Shared interface artwork continues to appear in the horizontal map.

Cartography brushes for GIMP

Cartography brushes for GIMP by yd, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0. Adapted by cropping, resizing, tinting, and compositing into Talk Quest map artwork.

Fantasy World Map

Fantasy World Map by yd, dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0. Paper, sea, coastline, wave, and grid layers were cropped and color-treated for three chapter maps.

Cartography and interface artwork

Kenney / Kenney Vleugels: Cartography Pack, UI Pack: RPG Expansion, and Game Icons. Dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0. Adapted by cropping, resizing, tinting, and compositing into map scenery and interface elements.
