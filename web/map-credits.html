Vocabulary picture library

Vocabulary cards combine Microsoft Fluent Emoji illustrations by Microsoft Corporation under the MIT License, Blender renders of Kenney models under CC0 1.0, adapted Mulberry Symbols, and the individually credited photographs and educational illustrations below.

We normalize the framing of Fluent illustrations and preserve their authored lighting. Kenney model adaptations include materials, clothing, poses, studio lighting and character animation. The models come from Kenney's Animated Characters: Protagonists, Food Kit, Furniture Kit and Toy Car Kit collections. Eight action pictures use joint-based Blender animation of these acquired models.

All 1,285 vocabulary pictures have an animated version. Alongside the eight articulated Blender clips, the remaining 1,277 illustrations use restrained motion suited to the pictured subject: gentle breathing, swaying, rigid movement, optical camera drift or moving surface light. Teaching arrows, comparisons and fixed supports retain their meaning. Photographs remain flat images; these adaptations do not add articulated limbs. Reduced-motion settings use the corresponding still picture. The 265 context-only words retain text-in-context practice without invented pictures.

The complete picture manifest records each word's creator, source, license, transformation and adapted output. The complete animation manifest records every animated output and its source credit, license and motion adaptation. The character and model source record and action animation record describe the corresponding adaptations.

Adapted teaching symbols

Mulberry Symbols are by Steve Lee, from the symbol design project originated by Garry Paxton, under CC BY-SA 4.0. We retain the educational shapes and comparison cues, soften outlines, moderate the palette, add gentle shading and adjust framing. Some compositions select or repeat source elements to clarify a word's meaning. Animated versions add subject-appropriate motion or local light emphasis while preserving teaching cues. These adapted still and animated illustrations remain under CC BY-SA 4.0.
