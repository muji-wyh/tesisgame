Proposed next · not implemented

More personality, not more interruptions.

Keep Pip's warm storybook identity. Borrow Duolingo's action-contingent response and contrast between small encouragements and rare milestones, not its artwork, fire transformation or pressure to return.

P0 · Touch

A little hello, then a high five

Respond to a deliberate tap within 80 ms. Offer a wing, wait for a real second tap, then land the contact and settle. Home and result companions only: never steal the header's menu action.

Needs: new contact choreography and a cue review. Existing six-layer art can be reused.

P0 · Awareness

Notice what the player is doing

A bounded head turn follows attention toward a word or input. Return to rest when the player leaves. Touch gets a brief acknowledgement, not permanent tracking.

Needs: authored gaze heads or a reviewed eye rig. Current baked pupils cannot actually track a pointer.

P1 · Learning

Share the effort and the win

Use a quiet nod for ordinary progress, supportive recovery for mistakes, and reserve the complete celebration for meaningful clears. Pronunciation and the real reward timeline keep priority.

Needs: event mapping and restrained variants. Do not alter scores, timings or reward ownership.

P2 · Discovery

Small, age-specific surprises

Baby can peek from the shell; older Pip can catch a slipping visor or offer a playful balance challenge. Occasional quiet moments, not another animation after every input.

Needs: any missing prop layers and age-specific keyframes. No placeholder feathers or hats presented as finished art.

Reference: Duolingo: Building character · Designing streak milestones. Their linked product GIF frame sequences were inspected; no audio or physical-device preference claim is made.

Sources, rights and review scope

All Pip pixels are the existing project-authored storybook PNG atlases and original Spring SVG atlas, identical to the game's material. Original Pip illustrator is not recorded; the repository declares ISC and records no separate illustrator license. No Duolingo artwork or audio is integrated.

Generated by tools/pip-growth/export-review.cjs: the actual native mascot APIs plus the maintained loading-screen Web Animations, sampled at 24 fps. Expressions and attention include held states, not a set of newly authored animations. Game audio, particles, learning and wallet operations are intentionally absent.

Regenerate with node tools\pip-growth\export-review.cjs. Source hashes and every supported age are included in the catalog. Full direction and acceptance criteria: docs/plans/2026-10-28-pip-interactions.md.
