# Game asset requirements

- Use appropriate sourced production assets for game elements, including characters, environments, props, treasures, interface artwork, visual effects, and audio. Do not substitute simple geometric shapes, flat colors, or generic procedural placeholders for the requested artwork.
- Find and inspect the corresponding assets before implementing their presentation. Record the original source, creator, license, acquisition status, and available animations. Clearly distinguish source previews from downloaded or integrated assets.
- Talk Quest creatures must be attractive, large, majestic, and friendly to children. Exclude frightening faces, horror designs, skull imagery, snarling expressions, and aggressive exposed teeth. Convey scale through the creature design, composition, and gentle motion.
- Follow [the asset art direction](docs/assets/art-direction.md) when choosing or replacing game assets.
